About me

I'm Hannan, a UX designer and researcher studying at Wilfrid Laurier University. I like taking complicated problems and turning them into experiences that feel simple, accessible, and actually useful to the people using them.

Why I do this

My aunt Isma is the reason I ended up in UX. She lives with a rare eye disability, and watching her struggle with everyday apps and websites that just weren't built with her in mind made me realize how much design actually matters.

That stayed with me. Now whenever I sit down to design something, I think about Isma first. If she can use it comfortably and without frustration, I'm on the right track.

"Good design is invisible. Bad design is a barrier."

My design approach

The same four steps, whether it's a client playbook or an Arduino box.

Understand

Research the people and the problem first. Interviews, surveys, usability tests, whatever actually gets me talking to real users.

Explore

Generate and compare different solutions before committing, so the first idea isn't automatically the one that ships.

Design

Turn ideas into interfaces people can actually use. Wireframes, prototypes, or a physical build, whichever fits what's being made.

Test and improve

Put it in front of real people again before calling it done, and refine based on what actually breaks.
